# Power BI Interactive Analytics Layer — CustomerLens

This document details the business architecture, data modeling strategy, key performance indicators, page design, drill-through workflows, and governance principles for the CustomerLens Power BI analytics application.

---

## 1. Executive Purpose & Scope

Power BI functions as the **executive visualization and commercial intelligence interface** for CustomerLens. It directly consumes precomputed, validated analytical datasets generated by upstream Python pipelines (Phases 4 through 10) and the PostgreSQL analytical layer (Phase 7).

### Core Boundaries & Principles
1. **Reporting & Exploration Layer:** Power BI acts strictly as a presentation and exploration tool. It does not recompute K-Means clusters, re-engineer RFM features, or modify transactional source records.
2. **Immutable Source Artifacts:** All source CSV files in `data/processed/` are treated as immutable, deterministic outputs.
3. **No Synthetic Demographics:** The underlying UCI Online Retail dataset contains no customer age, gender, occupation, or household income attributes. No demographic features are synthesized or implied.
4. **Descriptive, Not Predictive:** Behavioral segmentation categorizes accounts based on observed transaction patterns. It does not predict churn probabilities, guarantee campaign ROI, or claim causal marketing impact.
5. **Hypothesis-Driven Recommendations:** Commercial recommendations are structured business hypotheses designed to be validated through controlled A/B experimentation.

## 4. Recommended Semantic Data Model

To optimize query performance, eliminate circular references, and avoid many-to-many evaluation ambiguity, the model implements a **Star / Constellation Schema**:

```
                         ┌───────────────────────┐
                         │    segment_summary    │
                         │     (DimSegment)      │
                         └───────────┬───────────┘
                                     │ 1
                                     │
                                     │ [cluster_id]
                                     │ Single (1 -> *)
                                     ▼ *
 ┌───────────────────────────┐   ┌───────────────────────┐
 │  marketing_opportunities  │◄──┤   business_segments   │
 │       (RefMarketing)      │1:1│     (DimCustomer)     │
 └───────────────────────────┘   └───────────┬───────────┘
                                             │ 1
                                             │
                                             │ [customerid] = [CustomerID]
                                             │ Single (1 -> *)
                                             ▼ *
                                 ┌───────────────────────┐
                                 │ customer_transactions │
                                 │   (FactTransactions)  │
                                 └───────────────────────┘

                                 ┌───────────────────────┐
                                 │  clustering_metrics   │
                                 │  (Disconnected Fact)  │
                                 └───────────────────────┘
```

### Key Relationship Specifications:
- **`segment_summary` $\rightarrow$ `business_segments`:** 1-to-Many on `cluster_id` (Single direction). Propagates segment descriptions and action categories down to individual customer accounts.
- **`business_segments` $\rightarrow$ `customer_transactions`:** 1-to-Many on `customerid = CustomerID` (Single direction). Propagates customer-level attributes to sales line items.
- **`marketing_opportunities`:** Linked 1-to-1 with `segment_summary` on `cluster_id` (or merged in Power Query during ingestion).
- **`clustering_metrics`:** Strictly **disconnected** diagnostic table. Contains theoretical $K=2..10$ diagnostics that do not map to individual sales or customers.

## 5. Refresh & Lifecycle Considerations

- **Cadence:** Batch/scheduled refresh. Because the underlying analytical pipeline processes historical e-commerce logs in batch cycles (via Python and PostgreSQL), the Power BI dataset is refreshed whenever upstream ETL runs.
- **Data Source Path:** Relative or parameter-driven local file paths (`File.Contents(...)`) in Power Query, facilitating smooth migration between local development and Power BI Service / Gateway environments.
- **Storage Mode:** **Import Mode** is recommended. The entire analytical model (392,692 fact rows and 4,338 customer rows) consumes less than 40 MB in VertiPaq compressed in-memory storage, delivering sub-second visual rendering without SQL DirectQuery latency.

## 6. Validated Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

All measures in Power BI are validated against project-wide invariants established in earlier phases:

| KPI Name | DAX Expression Summary | Verified Baseline | Business Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Total Revenue** | `SUM(customer_transactions[Revenue])` | **£8,887,208.89** | Gross sales turnover across completed orders. |

## 7. Dashboard Architecture & Navigation

The application consists of **4 dedicated, thematic pages**:

### Page 1 — Executive Overview
- **Header:** 5 KPI cards displaying Total Revenue, Active Customers, Total Orders, AOV, and Repeat Customer Rate.
- **Monthly Revenue Velocity:** 13-month dual-axis chart showing revenue and completed order volume. Highlights the November 2011 seasonal holiday peak (£1.16M across 2,657 orders) and notes the partial December 2011 period (ending 9 December 2011).
- **Geographic Footprint:** Top 10 countries by turnover (United Kingdom accounts for 88.80% / £7,308,391.55; Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, France comprise the core international presence).
- **Pareto Concentration:** Clear visual demonstration that 65.6% repeat buyers generate 93.1% of turnover.
- **Executive Takeaways:** Plain-language synthesis emphasizing customer retention and repeat purchase habituation over top-of-funnel acquisition.

### Page 2 — Customer Segmentation
- **Segment Performance Scorecard:** High-contrast matrix displaying customer count, customer %, revenue, revenue %, concentration multiplier, and median Recency, Frequency, and Monetary spend.
- **Customer Share vs. Revenue Share:** Clustered bar chart displaying the structural revenue disparity between personas.
- **Revenue Concentration Multiplier:** Horizontal bar chart illustrating the **3.94x** concentration ratio of *High-Value Engaged* customers versus **0.17x** for *Low-Engagement* accounts.
- **Behavioral Separation:** Median Recency, Frequency, and Monetary spend comparisons.
- **Model Selection & Diagnostics:** Dual-axis visual depicting Silhouette Scores and Inertia across $K \in [2, 10]$, accompanied by a transparent narrative explaining why $K=4$ was selected as a business-oriented segmentation solution.

### Page 3 — RFM Analysis
- **Educational Banners:** Definitions, dataset medians, and quartile distributions for Recency (median: 51.0 d), Frequency (median: 2.0 orders), and Monetary spend (median: £668.57).
- **Distribution Histograms:** Visualizing positive right skews in Recency, Frequency, and Monetary spend.
- **Quintile Scoring Structure:** Visual breakdown of discrete R, F, and M quintile scores (1 to 5).
- **Log-Scale Scatter Visual:** Recency vs. Monetary spend scatter plot colored by persona, illustrating cluster boundaries.
- **Scope Guardrail:** Explicit reminder that RFM reflects observed historical transaction timing and does not predict future churn.

### Page 4 — Marketing Opportunities
- **4 Strategic Strategy Cards:**
  - *Protect & Grow (High-Value Engaged):* 716 accounts, £5.78M revenue. Focus on dedicated B2B account management, SLA protection, and margin guardrails.
  - *Nurture (Established Valuable):* 1,170 accounts, £2.09M revenue. Focus on order-threshold incentives (£500+) and category cross-selling.
  - *Develop (Recent Developing):* 834 accounts, £465.6K revenue. Focus on 30-day post-purchase automated onboarding and second-order conversion.
  - *Reactivate (Low-Engagement):* 1,618 accounts, £547.8K revenue. Focus on low-cost automated win-back clearance emails and list opt-outs.
- **Controlled Experimentation Matrix:** Structured table mapping target segments to treatment strategies, holdout control groups, primary KPIs, and guardrail metrics.
- **Scientific Integrity Guardrail:** Explicit disclaimer that marketing strategies are empirical hypotheses requiring randomized controlled testing before operational rollout.

## 8. Slicers, Filtering Hierarchy & Drill-Through Possibilities

### Global & Page-Level Slicers
- **`InvoiceDate` (Date Range Slider):** Global time filter spanning `2010-12-01` to `2011-12-09`.
- **`Country` (Dropdown / Search):** Filters sales and customers across 37 validated countries.
- **`business_segment` / `action_category` (Buttons / Tiles):** Slices segmentation and RFM visuals by persona.

### Drill-Through Scenarios
1. **Segment Summary $\rightarrow$ Customer Account Detail:** Right-clicking a segment on Page 2 drills through to a detailed table of customer accounts belonging to that segment, showing individual `customerid`, RFM scores, lifetime spend, and last purchase date.
2. **Customer Account $\rightarrow$ Transaction Line Items:** Right-clicking an individual customer account drills through to their complete invoice transaction history in `customer_transactions`.

## 9. Data Integrity & Analytical Invariants

The Power BI implementation enforces strict compliance with repository-wide data integrity benchmarks:

1. **Active Customers Invariant:** Exactly **4,338** unique customer accounts. All records have non-null, validated customer IDs.
2. **Completed Orders Invariant:** Exactly **18,532** unique invoice numbers (`InvoiceNo`).
3. **Turnover Invariant:** Exactly **£8,887,208.89** gross revenue.
4. **Average Order Value Invariant:** Exactly **£479.56**.
5. **Observation Window Invariant:** 1 December 2010 to 9 December 2011. December 2011 is documented as a partial month (9 days).
6. **No Predictive Fabrications:** No predictive churn probabilities, customer lifetime value forecasts, or guaranteed campaign uplift metrics are presented.
7. **No Synthetic Demographics:** No demographic variables exist in the dataset; all segmentation is purely behavioral.
8. **No Product Taxonomy:** Because UCI product descriptions lack formal hierarchical categories, product analysis is strictly line-item and item-level.
